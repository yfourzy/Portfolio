About

Entry-level game developer and designer with 3 years of team game development experience across Unity (C#), Roblox Studio (Luau), GameMaker, and Blender. I directed an original game project, programmed gameplay mechanics, and integrated art, audio, and gameplay work into team builds.

I work where the pieces meet: concept art, 3D models, sound effects, and gameplay code all have to land in the same build. I coordinate shared files, report progress in weekly check-ins, and let playtests decide what to tune next. So far that adds up to 2 games released on itch.io and 2 in development.

How I Integrate

Game integration is where my art, audio, and code experience meet. This is how a feature moves from idea to playable build.

Assets

Concept art, 3D models, and sound effects arrive from shared team files.

Mechanics

Gameplay is scripted in Luau and C# and tuned inside the engine.

Build

Everything is coordinated into one team build, with progress reported in weekly check-ins.

Playtest

QA playtests, bug logs, and feedback decide the next round of iteration.
